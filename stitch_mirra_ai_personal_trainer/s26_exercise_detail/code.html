Mirra Intelligence

Retract scapulae into bench; drive through heels and touch lower sternum before lockout.

Execution Protocol

5 Precision Steps
1
Setup & Eye Alignment

Lie flat with eyes directly beneath the bar. Plant feet firmly flat on the floor with moderate arch.

2
Grip & Lat Tension

Grip slightly wider than shoulder width. Squeeze the barbell firmly to pre-engage lats and triceps.

3
Unrack & Stabilize

Inhale deep into the diaphragm, brace abdominal core, and unrack with arms locked over upper chest.

4
Controlled Eccentric Descent

Lower bar steadily (2–3 seconds) until lightly touching lower sternum with elbows tucked at 45–60°.

5
Explosive Concentric Press

Drive heels down and press explosively up back toward eye line, exhaling past the sticking point.
