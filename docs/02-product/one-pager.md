# Mirra — one-pager

## One-liner

> Snap a mirror selfie. Get your week. Miss a day — your AI coach rebuilds it before you quit.

Hinglish: *Selfie maaro, week mil jaaye. Skip kiya? AI turant plan badal de.*

## Hook (acquisition)

**Mirror → Week** — 1 selfie + short Q&A → 60s personalized workout + diet week. Demoable on TikTok/Reels.

## USP (why switch)

Not another static plan app. **Proactive accountability agent** that notices skips and **rebuilds** the week (Future-feeling, AI price).

## ICP

US 18–28, wants results without $199/mo human coach; lives on Reels; will pay ~$8–13/mo effective annual.

## Pricing (target)

$12.99/mo or $79.99/yr via RevenueCat. 7-day trial.

## Core loop

1. Onboarding Q&A + mirror photo  
2. Week plan (diet + workouts)  
3. Daily AI check-in  
4. Skip → auto-reschedule / rebuild  
5. Weekly progress photos (trend, not medical BF%)  
6. Paywall  

## Do not lead with

“AI body fat %”, generic ChatGPT chat, India-first monetization.
