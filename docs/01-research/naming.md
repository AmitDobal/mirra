# Naming research (Gen Z hookable)

Date: 2026-10-30

## Criteria

Short (≤2 syllables ideal), easy to say on TikTok, signals **accountability / lock-in culture** or **mirror → plan**, App Store + domain freer than dead generics (Fit/Flex/Pulse).

## Rejected: LockIn / LockedIn

Gen Z slang **#LockIn** fits the USP — but App Store already crowded:

- [Training & Gym App: Lock In](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/training-gym-app-lock-in/id6738963949) (fitness + meal + app blocking)
- LockedIn FIT, LockIn screen-time apps

**Do not ship as LockIn.** Confusion + ASO fight.

## Shortlist

| Name | Why Gen Z | Risk | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mirra** | Mirror selfie hook baked into brand; soft, modern, Reel-friendly | Confirm `.com` / App Store | **Recommended working** |
| **Pact** | Daily pact with AI coach; commitment culture | Generic English; check TM | Strong alt |
| **Rebuild** | Skip → rebuild week = USP in the name | Less “cool”, more producty | Strong alt |
| **Clutch** | Sports Gen Z slang (“clutch up”) | Other brands; gaming feel | Maybe |
| **Reslot** | Unique verb for reschedule-on-skip | Invented; needs education | Maybe |
| Pulse / Rise / Forge | Common name lists | Overused | Skip |

## Working decision

**Ship docs + repo as `mirra`.** Confirm before App Store:

1. App Store search US/UK for exact “Mirra”
2. Domain + Instagram / TikTok handle
3. If blocked → fall back **Pact** then **Rebuild**

## Tagline options

- Mirra — *Mirror in. Week out.*
- Mirra — *Skip once. Rebuild tonight.*
- Mirra — *Your AI coach that doesn’t ghost.*
